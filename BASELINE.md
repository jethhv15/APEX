## Development Rule

Every future commit MUST satisfy at least one of the following:

- Evidence from Clover 3.11
- Benchmark improvement
- Bug fix
- Engine optimization
- Rule refinement
- Verification improvement

No new framework modules unless required by verified evidence.

## Goal

Build the fastest and most stable Android performance framework based on measurable evidence rather than assumptions.
